The problem

AI agents are ready to do real work. Teams aren't ready to let them.

Give an agent real access, and one wrong step, or one instruction hidden in an email, does damage that can't be undone.
Make a person approve every step, and the agent is slow, and after the fiftieth pop-up nobody reads what they approve.

So most agents stay read-only.

Here's the fix↓

Our solution

AI is the driver. Countersign is the brakes.

And the reverse gear. Countersign sits between your agents and the systems they touch, so agents can move fast without anything going wrong for good.

  • Agents work at full speed on a staged copy
  • Only steps that can't be undone wait for a person
  • Anything can be undone, even after it lands

It doesn't need an LLM. Every decision comes from your own database, cluster and cloud.

Every task is a transaction.

Agents don't get raw access. They get a layer that keeps the way back for everything they do, and shows you the exact result before it becomes real.

Stage

Nothing changes until you say so.

Database writes, file edits, shell commands and git pushes are staged. Messages wait in an outbox. Your real systems don't move while the agent works.

Preview

See exactly what will happen.

One preview shows every row, file and resource the task would change, with the diff, before anything is real.

Approve

A person decides what can't be undone.

Payments, mass deletes and anything sent to someone the user never named wait for a reviewer. One signed decision approves the exact preview.

Commit or undo

All of it, or none of it. Even later.

The whole task lands as one unit. Change your mind after? Undo puts back exactly what the task changed, and keeps what everyone else did since.

An engine, not another AI.

Countersign never asks a model whether something is safe. It is a deterministic engine: every decision comes from your systems themselves, so the same action gets the same answer every time, in milliseconds.

Your systems decide

Your database, your cluster and your cloud permissions say what an action really does. Nothing is guessed from the wording of a command.

Agents can't go around it

Run the agent inside Countersign and its only way out is through the layer. Direct file writes, network calls and secrets are closed off by the operating system.

Secrets stay secret

Passwords, keys and tokens are hidden from the agent and can't leave in a message, a push or an API call, even in pieces.
